Supporting Document Dossier | Section 2: Phase 1 – Los Angeles Training & Mentorship

Applicant: Sadie Lea Minifee
Artform: Dance & Choreographic Direction

📍 Key Hollywood Dance Institutions & Masterclass Syllabus

Millennium Dance Complex (North Hollywood)

Masterclass Focus: Advanced choreographic composition, complex group dynamics, fast-paced phrase execution, and industry staging methods.

Playground LA (West Hollywood – Melrose)

Masterclass Focus: High-impact musicality, street-jazz, foundational hip-hop textures, and dynamic performance textures under premier choreographers.

TMilly Studio (North Hollywood)

Masterclass Focus: Specialized movement direction for digital camera frames, lens perspective blocking, and cinematic lighting interaction.

mL Studio & Kinjaz Dojo (Los Angeles)

Masterclass Focus: Micro-musicality, spatial synchronization, house/locking foundations, and authentic urban movement lineage.

🗓️ Two-Week Intensive Immersion Schedule (Daily Breakdown)

WEEK 1: Advanced Technical Immersion & Lineage Reconnection
  • Days 1–3: 3x daily masterclasses at Millennium & Playground LA; analyzing choreographic transitions, ensemble spacing, and musical phrasing.
  • Day 4: TMilly Studio camera direction lab; observing camera tracking with live movement.
  • Day 5–7: Reconnecting with foundational street dance communities; studying studio warm-up facilitation, class management, and dancer wellbeing.
WEEK 2: Directorial Mentorship, Screen Choreography & Score Development
  • Days 8–10: 1-on-1 mentorship sessions with established LA choreographers focusing on rehearsal directing, notation, and pitching creative treatments.
  • Days 11–12: Screen movement directing lab; framing live movement for multi-camera setups.
  • Days 13–14: Synthesizing choreographic tasks, improvisation scores, and rehearsal syllabus to bring directly into the Welsh Phase 2 Studio R&D Lab.

🎯 Specific Upskilling & Sector Value for Wales

This immersion provides access to high-calibre choreographic composition and camera movement direction that is currently unavailable within Wales. By reconnecting with the Hollywood dance ecosystem with over a decade of professional performance experience, Sadie will translate cutting-edge global methodologies into the Welsh cultural ecology.

3 Contracted Professional Welsh Dancers

Paid Fair Industry Rates: £180/day (Aligned with Equity & Artists' Union England standards = £2,160 total). Recruited from the diverse Welsh dance community.

🔬 Studio R&D Research Framework (Core Objectives)

1. Movement Generation & Aesthetic Fusion
Fusing advanced California training, Ballroom/Voguing lines, and House grooves with contemporary floorwork to develop a distinct signature choreographic vocabulary for Wales.
2. Movement Direction for Screen & Camera Framing
Building on experience from In These Stones and Erykah Badu showcase to test how camera lenses, lighting angles, costume textures, and spatial framing transform dance narratives.
3. Documentation & Portfolio Asset Creation
High-quality filming of R&D movement studies to compile Sadie's first official Movement Direction and Choreography portfolio for commissioning bodies, screen producers, and theatres.
4. Informal Peer Sharing & Community Feedback
Hosting an informal work-in-progress sharing session for Cardiff dance artists, peers (including regular collaborator Leighton Rees), and community creatives to review and evaluate findings.

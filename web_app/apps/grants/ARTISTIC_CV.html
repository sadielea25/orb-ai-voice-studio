Artistic Profile

Accomplished Mixed-Caribbean, Queer dance artist and emerging Movement Director based in Cardiff with a BA (Hons) Dance degree (Kingston University London) and certified Street Dance Teaching Qualification. Over a decade of international performance, screen, and choreographic credits spanning premier global stages, major television networks (Netflix, BBC, ITV), national arts organizations (National Theatre Wales), cinematic screen projects (In These Stones – WMC Commission / ACW Funded), and headline music artists including Erykah Badu, Tinie Tempah, Martin Garrix, and Black Coffee. Featured by Dazed Magazine celebrating the Welsh Queer dance community. Deeply rooted in authentic street dance lineage through direct training with founding pioneers within the hip-hop community. Entirely self-funded throughout a decade-long career; currently undertaking a definitive career transition into independent Choreography and Movement / Art Direction in Wales.
